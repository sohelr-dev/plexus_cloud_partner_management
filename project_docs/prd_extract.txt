Partner Management Module
Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Module: Partner Management
Version: 1.0
Status: Development Ready
Document Type: Functional & Technical Product Requirements
Primary Users: Management, Partner Management, Sales, Marketing, Finance, Accounts, Network Operations, Inventory, Support Center Management, System Administrator

1. Module Overview
The Partner Management Module will provide a centralized system for managing the complete commercial, operational, financial, network, and business relationship between the company and its partners.
The module will support multiple partner business models while maintaining a single partner master record.
The module will manage:
? Partner registration
? Partner approval
? Partner business models
? Partner profile
? Business information
? Marketing analysis
? Customer growth
? Bandwidth sales
? Bandwidth allocation
? Bandwidth upgrade/downgrade
? Network equipment
? End devices
? Commission
? Partner-owned Support Centers/Branches
? Revenue
? Cost
? Payment
? Outstanding
? P&L
? ROI
? Documents
? Agreements
? Partner history
? Partner health
? Risk analysis
? Reports
? Audit logs
The system must connect partner-related information from existing ERP, billing, finance, network, inventory, customer, and other operational systems wherever applicable.

2. Main Objectives
The module must allow the company to:
1. Create and manage partners.
2. Define one or multiple business models for each partner.
3. Manage partner commercial relationships.
4. Monitor partner sales and marketing performance.
5. Manage bandwidth sales and allocation.
6. Track bandwidth upgrades and downgrades.
7. Manage partner-associated network equipment.
8. Manage partner end devices.
9. Configure and manage commission.
10. Manage partner-owned Support Centers/Branches.
11. Track partner revenue and cost.
12. Calculate P&L.
13. Calculate ROI.
14. Track payments and outstanding balances.
15. Maintain partner documents and agreements.
16. Maintain complete partner history.
17. Identify partner risks.
18. Provide management-level reports and insights.

4. Partner Business Models
The system must support the following business models.
4.1 Bandwidth Sales
Partners purchase or consume bandwidth/services from the company.
Examples:
? Internet
? GGC
? FNA
? BDIX
? Other network services
4.2 Commission Based
Partners earn commission based on configurable business rules.
Examples:
? Percentage of revenue
? Fixed amount
? Per activation
? Per customer
? Per renewal
? Package based
? Bandwidth based
4.3 End Device Based
The relationship is based on network endpoint devices.
Examples:
? MAC Address
? Router
? ONU
? ONT
? CPE
? Device ID
? Serial Number
4.4 Support Center
The partner operates one or more physical/operational Support Centers or Branches.
It represents the partner's own branch/customer-service/support operation.

5. Multiple Business Models
One partner can have multiple business models.
Example:
ABC Telecom

Business Models:
? Bandwidth Sales

? Support Center
